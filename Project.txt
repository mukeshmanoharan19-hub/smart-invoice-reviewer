User Story:
    As a finance administrator at an Indian company, I want to upload a multilingual invoice or receipt and receive a prepared review containing the best combined extraction, GSTIN and policy results, and a suggested GL account so I can approve valid documents quickly and turn supplier errors into a clear correction request.
    Note: Read that carefully. Client does not want an autonomous system that approves invoices. They wants a prepared review. The app does the reading, checking, and suggesting; they keeps the decision. That distinction drives everything that follows.

What the app must do:
    The scope for this build:

    Accept one PDF or image per upload, with a 4 MB limit
    Recognize whether the document is an invoice or a receipt automatically
    Extract supplier and customer details, GSTINs, dates, purchase order, currency, totals, and line items
    Validate Indian GSTIN formats offline and reconcile subtotal, tax, and total
    Apply separate deterministic policies for invoices and receipts, including duplicate detection
    Suggest a GL account from a fixed Northstar catalog, with the reviewer able to override
    Let Maya correct fields, approve, reject, and draft (never send) a supplier correction email
    Keep a review history with explicit deletion so a demo can be reset

    Just as important is what stays out. No authentication, no queues, no email ingestion, no accounting-system integration. This is a teaching skeleton that keeps the engineering boundaries visible, not a production accounts-payable product.
